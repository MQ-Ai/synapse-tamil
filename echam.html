அடைமொழி / எச்சம்

Section A3: five sentences, pick the word form that fits, 10 marks. Most A3 questions give four forms of the same verb. The word right after the blank tells you which form to choose.

Mastered
Look right, then choose. Cover the options. Read the word straight after the blank and ask: is it a thing, an action, a try / want, or how? That tells you the ending. Only then look at the options.

How this trains the brain

  • Name the pattern first. When the options are forms of one verb, you first say what comes after the blank. Explaining why before answering builds a rule you can reuse, not a guess.
  • Real exam sentences. 25 questions come from five 2026 papers, with the examiners' own wrong options.
  • Mixed practice. The four patterns come in random order, so you learn to tell them apart instead of repeating one rule.
  • Spacing and a confidence check. Missed sentences return three cards later. A lucky guess is not counted as mastered.
