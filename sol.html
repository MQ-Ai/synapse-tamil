How this trains the brain

  • Both directions. Word → meaning builds reading; meaning → word builds the recall you need when writing.
  • Look-alike choices. Wrong options are real meanings from the same list, so you must know the word, not guess from shape.
  • Spacing and a confidence check. Missed words return three cards later. A lucky guess is not counted as mastered.
  • Phrases by feeling. Composition phrases are grouped by the emotion or scene they describe, the way you will look for them while writing.
