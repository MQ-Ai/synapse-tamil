எட்டு வேற்றுமைகள்

All 8 Tamil case markers with suffixes, roles, and examples

# வேற்றுமை உருபு (suffix) Role in sentence Example

Exam tip: Read the blank's position relative to the verb. The verb tells you what role the noun plays, subject (1st), object (2nd), instrument (3rd), recipient (4th), ablative (5th), genitive (6th), locative (7th), or vocative (8th).

Sandhi note: Many suffixes change with the final phoneme of the noun: ஐ → யை after vowels; இல் / இலிருந்து → ல் / லிருந்து after vowel stems. Watch the junction carefully.
