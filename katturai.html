கட்டுரை வழிகாட்டி

Snap your composition question, then build the story one step at a time: understand it, plan it, gather phrases, write, and check it against what markers look for. Your work saves on this device as you go.

How this builds a better composition

  • Think before you write. Answering who, where, when, what went wrong and what was learnt first keeps the story on the question.
  • One paragraph, one job. Every picture or event gets its own paragraph, so nothing is left out.
  • Collect, then use. Phrases from the phrase bank and a proverb are chosen before writing, and the write step ticks them off as you use them.
  • Check like a marker. The last step checks content, language and organisation, partly automatically and partly by you.
