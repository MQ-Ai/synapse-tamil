Terms and privacy

Last updated 5 October 2026. Questions: sage.synapse@gmail.com

Passes

  • After signing in, the first round in each lab is free. A pass opens every round and every exam paper in all labs.
  • Exam-year pass: S$49, valid until 31 December 2027. 30-day pass: S$12, valid for 30 days from payment.
  • Both are one-off payments by PayNow or card, handled by Stripe. Nothing renews automatically.
  • A pass covers one pupil and up to three devices. Further devices can be unlocked by emailing us.
  • Pupils under 18 should ask a parent or guardian to pay.

Refunds

If you are not happy, email us within 7 days of paying for a full refund, no questions asked. After that, we refund only if the site was unavailable for a long time. A refunded pass stops working on its devices within a day.

What we collect and why

  • Sign-in: name, school, level and an email address (the pupil's or a parent's). We use these to know who uses Synapse Tamil, to improve it, and to reply to you.
  • Usage: pages opened, rounds finished and scores, linked to a random device id. We use this to see which labs help and what to improve.
  • Payment: the email given at checkout, the pass bought, the amount and date. We use this to unlock the pass and to handle refunds. Card and bank details go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Reminders (only if you switch them on): the browser gives us an address for delivering notifications to that device, linked to a random device id. It contains no name or phone number. We use it only to send the daily challenge reminder. Switch reminders off on the challenge page or in your phone settings at any time.
  • Learning progress, including your streak, stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

The data is kept in a private Google Sheet that only the Synapse team can open. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, and we do not show advertising.

Children

Most of our users are primary school pupils. We ask pupils to sign in with a parent's email where possible, and passes should be bought by a parent or guardian. A parent who pays agrees to these terms for their child.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep sign-in and usage data while the pupil uses Synapse, and payment records for as long as the law requires for accounts. To see, correct or delete your data, email sage.synapse@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
